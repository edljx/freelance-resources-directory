## Rate Calculators

### Free Calculators

#### Freelance Rate Calculator 🆓 ⭐
- Calculate your hourly rate
- **Website:** https://www.hellobonsai.com/freelance-rate-calculator

#### Creative Rate Calculator 🆓
- For designers and creatives
- **Website:** https://www.creativelive.com/blog/free-freelance-rate-calculator/

#### Hourly to Annual Calculator
- **Website:** https://www.calculator.net/salary-calculator.html

### Rate Calculation Formula

```
Hourly Rate = (Annual Salary Goal + Business Expenses + Taxes) 
              / (Billable Hours × 1.2 margin)

Example:
Goal Salary: $80,000
Expenses: $10,000
Taxes (~30%): $27,000
Total Needed: $117,000
Billable Hours: 1,200/year (25 hrs/week × 48 weeks)
Rate: $117,000 / 1,200 = ~$98/hour
```

- **Website:** https://www.rocketlawyer.com/

### Essential Documents

1. **Master Service Agreement (MSA)** - Overall working relationship
2. **Statement of Work (SOW)** - Project-specific details
3. **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)** - Confidentiality
4. **Copyright Assignment** - Intellectual property transfer
